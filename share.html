Why is my file missing?
  • The share sheet only lists AutoPort after the PWA is installed (Add to Home screen).
  • Chrome filters by the MIME type the sending app reports. A file manager that reports nothing ('') or application/octet-stream is matched because both are in accept; a type outside the list (e.g. application/zip) will not be.
  • After changing share_target in the manifest you must uninstall and reinstall the PWA — the share registration is captured at install time.
